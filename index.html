AI Content Factory

An independently developed content intelligence and original video production system.

About the Project

AI Content Factory is a development-stage software project created to assist with content research, content intelligence, and original video production.

The system analyzes publicly available content signals to help identify subjects that may be suitable for further research and original content creation.

YouTube Data API Usage

AI Content Factory uses the YouTube Data API to discover recent public videos and retrieve publicly available video metadata and statistics for content research and trend analysis.

Data may include information such as video titles, publication times, view counts, like counts, and comment counts made available through the YouTube Data API.

The application uses these signals to analyze content trends, compare related topics, evaluate content opportunities, and prioritize subjects for independent research.

Videos discovered through the YouTube Data API are used as research and trend signals. AI Content Factory does not download or redistribute YouTube videos as part of this analysis.

Original Content

Topics selected by the system undergo additional research and fact-checking before original scripts and video assets are produced. The objective is to create original content rather than reproduce content discovered through YouTube.

Current Availability

AI Content Factory is currently a private, locally operated, development-stage application used by its independent developer. The software itself is not currently offered as a public service.
